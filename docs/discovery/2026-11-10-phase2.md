## The general mechanism: profile action entries

Every action a person link offers (right-click menu, profile "more" menus) is also in the **profile's secondary tabs**:
`OverviewTitleBar > SecondaryTabsList` backing rows (`ListRead.Items`) carry, under nested `ChildList`s, rows whose
`RowData` holds an `ActionEntry` record:
`{Text (localized), ID (FM.GamePlugin.ActionID, int), Event (uint), ActionData (record), ChildList, Disabled, ...}`.

- Folders have `Event` 0 and children; leaves that perform have `Event` 44 (seen for every leaf) or 0 for
  single-step actions (approach, offer contract).
- `ActionData` names the target (`ActionDataReferences[]` with `{NewsItemActionParameter, NewsItemActionDataReference: Person|Club}`;
  the profile person, the human club and the manager) and leaf parameters (e.g. a shortlist duration in months).
- The entry list refreshes by itself after an action (e.g. "add to shortlist" is replaced by "remove from shortlist";
  scout assignment is replaced by a disabled "scouting in progress" entry, `ActionID 1467054661`).
- Record access: `Record.TryGetValue(PropertyIdentifierSet id)`; a row value is often wrapped as
  `{DisplayValue, PropertyValue}`, so look inside `PropertyValue` (Spike `Field`).

**Perform** = the game's own dispatcher, the call the menu makes:
`FM.Game.PluginContextActionsModule.HandleActionEvent((ActionID)id, entry.Event, actionRef, entry.ActionData,
checkForConfirmation: true, source: null)`. `actionRef` is the `FM.UI.ActionReference` that the subtab system binds at
root node `subtab-action<N>.game.Action`; its persistent identity ends with `:<ActionID>` (identity
`825307168:1,1198877028:<n>,1633904996:<ActionID>`). With `checkForConfirmation: true` the game shows its own
`GenericModalDialog` when the entry carries `ConfirmationDialogData`; the op then clicks the dialog's
`buttons-button-primary-default` SIButton (`PrimaryButtonText` is in the entry, so the label is not needed).

## Q1 staff.offer (F11)

Target: a free-agent staff member from Staff Search for a recruitment job with open slots (`fm staff vacancies`).

Route:
1. `profile_open {uid, kind: nonplayer}` (1.2 s).
2. Perform `PersonApproachToSign` (1.8 s) → `ContractNegotiationTool` opens with a negotiation
   (`Person.ContractEditSession`, a `ContractEditSessionReference`).
3. The staff member's **demand** is the prefilled offer ("this is the contract I want", *localized* bubble): job =
   their preferred job, wage, 2 years. This is the wage demand that Staff Search does not expose.
4. Job: `primary-job-term` row → `inputs-combobox-default-default` (SIDropdown) → `@FloatingElement` rows: job
   groupings (`PrimaryJobPossibleGroupings`), each a `ChildDropdown` sub-menu with the jobs; click the `DropdownRow`.
   `SelectedJobIsRecruitment` etc. confirm the group.
5. Wage: `wage-term` row → `SILongField` (typed value) and SIButtons `-` / `+`, which step through
   `WeeklyWageComponentInfo.CashSelectionIntervalsInfo.IntervalValues` (base-currency values). The board's maximum
   is the hard ceiling: above it the component has `InvalidOrDisabledReason` (*localized* "exceeds board maximum"),
   `CanIncrementValue` False, and the propose action is disabled. One `-` from the demand landed exactly on the
   board maximum (last interval value).
6. Propose = the speak-to action with `Event 20` (`SpeakTo.Actions.ActionsForButtonsList[]`, *localized* title;
   `IsDisabled` tells whether it can be sent). Button: `StandardButton` in `ContractNegotiationTool`, matched to the
   action list by order. Cancel = `IsExitButton` True, `Event 811955833`, with confirmation dialog id
   `ContractCancelNegotiations`.

Result (1 offer, wage at the board maximum, about half the demand): **answered immediately** (2.5 s): the staff member
countered. The counter is written into the session: `Wage`, `PrimaryJob` (back to their preferred job,
`PrimaryJobComponentInfo.BaseComponentData.State` 2 + `DisableLock`), `BaseDetailsTabChangedByOtherParty` True,
`NegotiationPatience {NegotiationPatienceStatus 0→2, NegotiationPatienceValue 100→10}`, plus *localized* bubbles
("too far from my demands, make an offer I can accept or talks end"; "giving me the job I asked for would make this
easier"). The counter was above the board maximum, so it cannot be accepted as is. Not pursued (owner rule).

Read-back (headless, no screen): person `ContractEditSession` with contexts **`human` = Human, `BuyingClub` =
Human.Club**, chain `Wage`, `WeeklyWageComponentInfo`, `PrimaryJob*`, `NegotiationPatience`,
`BaseDetailsTabChangedByOtherParty`, `NegotiationReadOnly`, `ContractNegotiationKeyInformation` (*localized*
summary lines), `ContractNegotiationRemainingWageBudget(WithContract)`. Before any approach the session reads as no
value. The session survives leaving the screen. `ContractOfferScreenValidation.ContractOfferScreenValid` (ctx
`human`) tells whether an offer screen may open.

Failure modes: demand above the board maximum (propose disabled until lowered); job dropdown order is per person;
patience can end talks; a counter can move the job back to the preferred one; no slot check by the game (the agent
must check `staff_slots` itself).

## Q4 training.apply_week + individual focus (F10)

Week template: `TrainingScreen` has one `TrainingScheduleSelectorPopup` (SIFloatingPopupWidget) per week row (order =
week order; bind `week.TrainingScheduleName` inside). Clicking it opens `@FloatingElement` with category rows
(`category-name`) and `schedule-list` rows (`apply-schedule` SIButton + `schedule-name`, bind
`row.DescriptionShort`). Clicking `apply-schedule` replaces the whole week (2 s) and keeps matches/travel slots.
Each future week also has `Copy` / `Paste` / `Clear` buttons (`button-secondary-label` inside; the current week and
the next one had none).

Proof: week of days 21–27 changed from template A to template B: 4 of 7 days changed `session_types`; `training` op
read it back headless-equivalent (it reads the screen's backing rows).

Restore: re-applying template A reproduced every slot but one (a day after a match came back as recovery `301`
instead of rest `300`), and the week label became "A (modified)" (*localized* suffix). Fixed the one slot via the
Phase 0 session card (`TrainingSessionSelectCard`: slot by `Event.SessionName` element, option radio
`item.DisplayValue`, `confirm`). Final state: all `session_types` and `sessions` equal the original; only the week
label keeps the "modified" suffix (the schedule name the game reads is now that of a customized week). Lesson:
template output depends on the day it is applied, so "restore" must be slot-by-slot from a snapshot (or the untested
`Copy` before / `Paste` after), never "re-apply the old template".

Individual focus: `TeamIndividualTrainingTool`, opened with `PanelManager.Open(guid 41012275212b88b46957d71fc17c5876,
"team", TeamReference(first team))` (param `player` opens it empty). Select the player's table row (`SelectionCell`,
backing rows = person refs, so a row can be matched to a uid), then `focus` → SIDropdown → `@FloatingElement`
`DropdownRow`s in the order of `PossibleIndividualTrainingTypes`.

Read-back (headless): person `PlayerTraining` (contexts `human`, `team`) → `AdditionalFocus` (context **`Judge` =
Human**) = `{IndividualTrainingTypeRow (id; -1 none), IndividualTrainingType {Name}, PossibleIndividualTrainingTypes[],
CanEdit, Available, HumanControlled}`. Proof: `-1 → 30 → -1` (one goalkeeper option and back). Language-neutral: the
row id and the list order; names are *localized*.

## Q5 contract.offer_renewal (F5)

Target: an own backup player, about 300 days left (no contract within 180 days in this save).

Route: own player profile → perform `PersonOfferContract` (1.8 s) → `ContractNegotiationTool`:
1. **Promises stage** (`Person…PreContractPromisesEditSession`, `PreContractPromisesEditSessionReference`):
   requested `AgreedPlayingTime` (here equal to the current squad status), start date; *localized* bubble; button
   "finalize promises" (`StandardButton`). Then "negotiate contract".
2. **Contract stage**: the prefilled offer is the player's **demand**: `Wage`, `LoyaltyBonus`, `AgentFee`, start, end
   (`EndDate`), `BonusesList` / `ClausesList` (`ContractEditSessionAddOnComponentReference`, e.g. appearance fee,
   clean-sheet bonus, unused-sub fee, one-year extension after n league matches), budget impact
   (`ContractNegotiationWageBudgetContribution`, `…ExistingWageContribution`, `…AverageWageForPlayingTime`,
   `…RemainingWageBudget`, *localized* money strings).
3. Decision: demand wage ≈ 1.2× current and far below the game's advised maximum for the status (`fm squad
   contracts` → `advised_max`) → submitted ONE offer = the demand ("finalize deal", `StandardButton` 0).

Result: **deferred answer**: *localized* "I'll come back about my final decision soon"; the only remaining action is
"exit talks". Read-back (headless, contexts `human`, `BuyingClub`): `ContractEditSession.NegotiationReadOnly`
`False → True`, `NegotiationPatience.NegotiationPatienceStatus 0 → 5`. `FullContract` unchanged (the decision comes
with a date advance, which we never do); `FullContract.HasRenewalOffer` stayed `False`, so it is not the marker.

`Estimated*` demands (`EstimatedWageDemands`, `EstimatedContractDemandsSummary`, …): **no data handler on any type in
the live tree even during this negotiation** (tree_probe, 60 types). In FM27 the demands are the prefilled session
terms at the "this is the contract I want" step; read those. The prefilled `Wage` at the promises stage already equals
the demand.

## Language dependence

Language-neutral: action ids, `Event` numbers, PanelID GUIDs, element names (`StandardButton`,
`buttons-button-primary-default`, `apply-schedule`, `TrainingScheduleSelectorPopup`, `DropdownRow`, `focus`,
`wage-term`, `primary-job-term`, `-`/`+` SIButtons), list orders, session types, training type row ids, base-currency
wage values, `NegotiationPatienceStatus`, `IsBeingScouted`, `IsShortlisted`. Localized (display only): every entry
`Text`, bubbles, key information lines, job names, schedule names, option names. Matching the speak-to action list to
`StandardButton`s is by order; production should verify the count matches `ActionsForButtonsList`.

## Failure modes and risks

- A subtab action id appears many times (folders + leaves); choosing by flattened index can hit the wrong leaf
  (e.g. another staff member's scouting folder). Walk the tree.
- Own-player profiles expose destructive actions next to safe ones (release, termination): allow-list ids.
- `checkForConfirmation: true` shows the game's dialog (good: same as the UI) and leaves it open if the op stops;
  an op must confirm or cancel it on every path.
- A negotiation, once opened, persists; leaving the screen does not cancel it. Cancel has its own confirmation
  (`ContractCancelNegotiations`) and may block a new negotiation for a while (*localized* warning).
- Staff counters can exceed the board maximum: then no acceptable reply exists without lowering demands.
- Renewal/staff answers can be immediate (staff) or deferred to a date advance (player renewal): "verified" must
  accept both (spec §8: "recorded, pending or answered").
- Week template restore is not exact (see Q4).
- Elements outside the window (y > window height) still receive synthetic clicks (`Copy` of the last week).

## Recommended op shapes

All `act_*` ops: plugin side requires the per-plan token, runs under `Nav.UiLock`, normal game screens only, checks
the target (viewed person = uid), performs, confirms its own dialog, reads back headless and returns `{before, after,
verified, answer?}`. No generic click op.

| op | args | result |
|---|---|---|
| `act_staff_offer` | `{uid, job_type, wage_value}` (base currency, ≤ board max) | `{demand: {job_type, wage, end_date}, offered: {...}, answer: "accepted"\|"countered"\|"rejected"\|"pending", counter: {wage, job_type, end_date}\|null, patience: {status, value}}` |
| `read_staff_demand` | `{uid}` (opens the approach; no offer) | `{job_type, wage, end_date, board_max_wage}` — opening = approaching, so it is an action too |
| `act_shortlist_add` / `act_shortlist_remove` | `{uid, months: 0\|1\|3\|6\|12}` / `{uid}` | `{shortlisted: bool, count}` |
| `act_scout_request` | `{uid, scout_uid, duration}` | `{being_scouted: true}` |
| `act_training_week` | `{week_index, schedule_index}` or `{slots: [{day, slot, session_type}]}` | `{days_before, days_after}`; restore = slots from a snapshot |
| `act_training_focus` | `{uid, type_row}` (`-1` = none) | `{type_row_before, type_row_after}` |
| `act_contract_renewal` | `{uid, accept_promises: true, wage_value?}` (default = demand) | `{promises, demand: {wage, loyalty_bonus, end_date, bonuses[], clauses[]}, answer: "pending"\|..., read_only}` |

Keep as manual steps for now: staff job selection when the wanted job is not in the person's offer list; bonuses and
clauses edits; counter-offers on either negotiation (new approval each, per §8); cancelling a negotiation; week
`Copy`/`Paste` (untested); any action id not in the table above.

## Spike ops (dev only, `Spike.cs`, behind `~/.fm-assistant/dev-nav`)

`ui_find {panel|@Root, name?, bind?, type?, within?, within_index?, y_min?, y_max?}`, `ui_click {… , index, wait_ms}`
(refuses MainMenu, OverviewTitleBar outside `SecondaryTabsList`, and names containing continue/save/proceed/match/
press/quit/exit/holiday), `ui_roots {level}`, `subtab_action {action_id, nth, perform}`, `person_panel_open {guid,
param, uid}`, `ref_info {path}` (InteropReference identity + property list). Production should not ship these.
